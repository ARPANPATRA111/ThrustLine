# Google Play listing

Text and answers for Play Console. Character limits are Google's; every field below fits.

## Store listing

**App name** (30 max)

```text
THRUSTLINE: Jetpack Shooter
```

**Short description** (80 max)

```text
Fly, fight and capture the flag in 8-player jetpack battles with your friends.
```

**Full description** (4000 max)

```text
Strap on a jetpack and fight over abandoned sky refineries above an acid sea. THRUSTLINE is a fast side-view shooter for up to 8 players, made to run well on budget phones too.

JUMP IN SECONDS
• Quick Play drops you into a live match straight away. CPU players fill empty seats, so there is always a fight.
• No sign-up and no account. Pick a callsign and play.
• Create a room and send the invite link to your group. Friends who tap it land in your room.

FLY AND FIGHT
• Analog jetpack: tap to hop, hold to fly, ease off to hover and drift.
• A pistol plus your pick of AR, SMG, shotgun or sniper, a knife, and frag, impact or EMP grenades.
• Shotgun recoil and grenade blasts throw you across the map. Chain them to move fast.
• Railgun airdrops, shields, health, ammo and grenade crates.
• Get three kills in a row and you become a glowing gold bounty.

MODES AND MAPS
• Free-for-all, or Red vs Blue teams from 1v1 to 4v4.
• Deathmatch, King of the Hill and Capture the Flag.
• Four loadouts: Arms, Blades (knives only), Boom (grenades only) and Snipers.
• Five maps: RIG-7, HELIOS-9, KILN-3, SKYREACH and CRYO-12.
• The final minute is FRENZY, and in the last 30 seconds the acid rises.
• Hosts set the house rules: round length, health, CPU skill, infinite fuel and ammo, pickups, friendly fire and more.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Twin-stick touch controls built for landscape.
• Gamepad support, and keyboard and mouse on Chromebooks.
• Remappable controls, a colour-blind palette, and camera shake and particle sliders.
• Level up to unlock sticker emotes, and climb the monthly season board.

LIGHT ON YOUR PHONE
• The app is under 1 MB.
• Graphics adjust to your device automatically.
• Game updates arrive the moment they ship, with no app update to install.

FAIR AND OPEN
• The server referees every match and checks each move and every hit.
• No ads and no in-app purchases.
• THRUSTLINE is open source: github.com/ARPANPATRA111/ThrustLine

THRUSTLINE needs an internet connection.
```

## App content

**Privacy policy:** `https://thrustline.pages.dev/privacy/`

The same answers are public at `https://thrustline.pages.dev/data-safety/`; keep both pages in step with this file.

**Ads:** No, the app contains no ads.

**App access:** All functionality is available without special access. There is no login.

**Content rating** (IARC questionnaire, category Game). What the game contains:

- Violence: stylised, cartoon soldiers shoot and knife each other. No blood or gore.
- Users interact: yes. Players see each other's callsigns and send preset stickers. There is no
  text or voice chat.
- Shares the user's location: no.
- Digital purchases: no. Gambling or simulated gambling: no.
- Sexual content, crude humour, drugs, alcohol, tobacco: none.

**Target audience:** 13 and over. The store listing does not target children.

**Data safety**

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does the app collect or share user data? | Yes, it collects data. Nothing is shared. |
| Is all data encrypted in transit? | Yes (HTTPS and secure WebSockets) |
| Account creation | The app does not let users create an account |
| Can users ask for their data to be deleted? | Yes, as described in the privacy policy |

| Data type | Collected | Why | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal info → Name (the callsign) | Yes | App functionality | No: a random callsign is given if the player picks none |
| Device or other IDs (the random player ID) | Yes | App functionality | Yes |
| App activity → Other actions (XP, kills, wins, season points) | Yes | App functionality | Yes |
| App info and performance → Other (connection ping) | Yes | App functionality | Yes |

None of this data is used for advertising, analytics or personalisation. Hosting by Vercel and
SpacetimeDB Maincloud counts as a service provider, not sharing.

**Advertising ID:** No. The app does not use it and does not declare the permission.

**Government, financial, health or news app:** No.
