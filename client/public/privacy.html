THRUSTLINE privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. Applies to the game at thrustline-drab.vercel.app and the THRUSTLINE Android app.

THRUSTLINE is a free, open-source multiplayer game. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page lists everything the game keeps and why.

What the game stores

  • A random player ID. Created the first time you play, so the server can tell players apart. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.
  • Your callsign. The name you choose (or a random one). Other players see it in matches and on the season board.
  • Your progress. XP and level, kills, deaths, wins, rounds played, best kills in a round, season points and when you last played. Other players can see this.
  • Match data. Your position, health, score and connection ping while you are in a room. It is deleted when you leave.
  • A transfer key. A random secret that lets your progress move with you if the game changes servers. Only the server and your device know it.

What stays on your device

Your login token, transfer key, settings and volume levels are saved in the browser storage for this site. In the Android app that is Chrome's storage for the site, so the app and Chrome share the same progress.

Who handles it

The website is hosted by Vercel and the game server runs on SpacetimeDB Maincloud, run by Clockwork Labs. Like any web service, they receive your IP address and basic request details in order to deliver the game. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

The Android app

The app opens this website in Chrome (or Android's built-in web view if Chrome is missing). It asks only for internet access and to check whether you are online. It collects nothing of its own.

Deleting your data

Clearing this site's data removes your ID from your device and starts you over. In the app, long-press the THRUSTLINE icon, open Site settings and clear the stored data. To have your profile removed from the server too, open an issue on GitHub with your callsign and level, and it will be deleted.

Children

THRUSTLINE is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome on GitHub.
