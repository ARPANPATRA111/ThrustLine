        HOW TO PLAY

        Free-for-all on RIG-7. Most score when the timer hits zero wins. Kill +100. Killing a gold bounty (3-kill streak) +50. Dying to acid or your own grenade −50.

        Keyboard & mouse: A/D move · W or Space thrust (tap = hop, hold = fly) · S drop through platforms · mouse aims · click fires · right-click or F knife · G grenade · Q swap · R reload · Tab scoreboard · Esc menu.

        Touch: left thumb moves (push up to fly, down to drop), right thumb aims and fires when pushed far. Buttons: swap, reload, grenade, knife.

        Modes: Arms (guns), Blades (knives, +15% speed), Boom (unlimited grenades). Shotgun recoil and grenade blasts throw you around: chain them with the jetpack. The last minute is FRENZY: 1.5× damage and faster fuel. Stay out of the green acid.
